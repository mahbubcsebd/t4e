How it works

One company. One workspace. One pool of credits.

Think New and Think Big are company accounts. Your projects, users and credits live together in your own workspace, so nothing is scattered across personal accounts.

1

Sign up

The person who signs up becomes the company administrator.

2

Get your workspace

Your company gets its own server. All of its projects, users and credits live there.

3

Buy a credit pack

Choose a pack when you need credits. Each project uses 90 credits a month, AI work uses credits as you go, and credits you buy roll over.

4

Add your team

On Think Big, the administrator adds company users, picks each person's role and chooses which projects they can open. Everyone draws from the shared credits.

Think Big

Built for teams that work on the same system

Separate accounts mean separate workspaces, split credits and projects nobody else can open. Think Big puts the whole team on one Living Blueprint.

One shared blueprint per project

Everyone works from the same concepts, flows, rules and screens, so designs don't drift apart.

Roles and per-project access

Give each person a role such as Developer, Project Manager or QA Engineer, and open only the projects they need. Deactivate anyone instantly.

Pooled credits with admin caps

Busy teammates draw more and quieter ones draw less, so no credits sit stranded in someone else's account.

Parallel coding with your tools

Each member connects their own Claude Code, Cursor or Codex session to the same project through the Think MCP server.

Bring in freelancers when you need them

Hire through the Think4Ever Marketplace and give freelancers scoped access to your projects that ends with the contract.

Project limits

Reached your project limit? You have options.

Every project in your workspace counts toward your plan's limit, whoever created it. Limits are fixed: 1 project on Think Free, 10 on Think New and 25 on Think Big. When you reach yours, you can:

Delete a project

Free up a slot by deleting a project you no longer need. Deleted projects are archived for 6 months and are no longer charged.

Upgrade your plan

Move from Think New to Think Big for up to 25 projects, or talk to us about Think Blue.

FAQ

Questions about plans

Is there a subscription?

No. Think Free, Think New and Think Big have no subscription. On Think New and Think Big, each project you keep uses 90 credits a month, and AI work uses credits as you go. You pay only for the credit packs you buy. Think Free's one project is free, and Think Blue is priced for each organization.

Why do projects use credits each month?

Each project runs in your company's hosted workspace. The monthly project credits cover hosting it, storing it and keeping it ready for your team.

When are project credits charged?

90 credits are taken from your credit pool when you create a project, and again every month after that. Credits aren't refunded for part of a month if you delete a project.

Do archived or deleted projects use credits?

No. Archived and deleted projects aren't charged. Deleted projects are archived for 6 months.

How do credit packs work?

Think New packs cost $25, $50, $75 or $100, at $0.05 per credit. Think Big packs cost $180, $360, $480 or $960, at $0.06 per credit. Buy a pack whenever you need more credits. Your projects and your AI work both draw from the credits you buy.

Why do Think Big credits cost more?

Think Big credits include what teams need to work together: a shared workspace and credit pool, up to 25 users, roles and per-project access, and admin controls.

Do unused credits roll over?

Credits you buy roll over from month to month until you use them. Think Free's 500 credits are a one-time grant and don't renew.

What happens when we run out of credits?

AI work pauses until you buy another pack. If there aren't enough credits for a project's monthly charge, that project is paused until you add credits. On Think Free, move to Think New or Think Big to get more credits.

What happens when we reach the project limit?

Project limits are fixed. Delete a project to start a new one, or upgrade to a plan with a higher limit. Deleted projects are archived for 6 months.

Who is the administrator?

The person who signs up. The administrator manages users, roles, project access and credits, and can see every project in the company workspace.

Do my team members need their own Think4Ever accounts?

No. On Think Big, the administrator creates company users inside your workspace with a login and a temporary password. Each user gets an email and sets their own password at first sign-in. They don't sign up or pay separately.

What can each team member see?

Each user sees only the projects the administrator assigned to them, plus any projects they created themselves. The administrator sees all company projects.

Who pays for AI usage?

All AI work by every user is paid from the company's shared credit pool. On Think Big, the administrator can set caps so no single user or project uses more than its share.

How do I remove someone's access?

The administrator can deactivate a user at any time, and that user loses access immediately.

Can I use my own coding tools?

Yes, on every plan. Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and other MCP-compatible tools can build from your Think4Ever design through the Think MCP server, on your own subscription. See How to: Design in Think, Code in Claude.

Can I bring my own model?

Yes. Every plan supports BYOK, so you can use supported provider credentials instead of putting all model usage into one bundle.

I'm on a Think New or Think Big subscription. What happens to my plan?

Your current plan stays in place for now. We'll contact you at least 60 days before anything changes, and show you what you'd pay under the new pricing.
