Role 01

Product Managers

Product owners, business analysts, delivery managers

“Will what ships match what we approved?”
In the Living Blueprint, open first
  • Requirements
  • Business flows
  • Business rules
  • UI designs
A short routine
  1. Approve requirements and untick what is out of scope.
  2. Walk the main flows and click through the UI preview with stakeholders.
  3. Review each rule's severity and on-violation behavior, then approve the build plan by phase.
What they get

What ships matches what was approved, with fewer late surprises and a traceable line from rule to code.

Approve the intent. Know it was built.

Role 02

Developers

Engineers working with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and other agents

“Why am I re-explaining this product to every agent?”
In the Living Blueprint, open first
  • Concept map & summaries
  • Data objects
  • Business rules
  • UI designs
A short routine
  1. Connect Claude Code, Cursor or Codex to the project once, over MCP.
  2. Ask the agent to pull the concept, schema and rules before writing code.
  3. On an inherited repo, read block summaries and flows first.
What they get

Less re-prompting and rework, agents that stop inventing schemas, and onboarding to unfamiliar code without reading it line by line, in the editor you already use.

Stop re-explaining your product to every agent.

Role 06

Quality Assurance (QA)

QA, security, risk and compliance

“What was this supposed to do, and can I prove it does?”
In the Living Blueprint, open first
  • Business flows
  • Business rules
  • Roles & permissions
  • Readiness report
A short routine
  1. Derive test cases from flow failure paths and rule violations.
  2. Test access one role at a time against the permissions matrix.
  3. Use data flow diagrams, security diagrams and the hardening report as review evidence.
What they get

Documentation, data flows and linked rules for code you did not write, and findings ranked by severity instead of buried in a scan log.

Review the system, not just the code changes.
