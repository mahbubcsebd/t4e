<p class="italic text-gray-500">Where AI-enabled freelancers and enterprises get work done.</p>
<p class="mt-4">The way software gets built has changed. Developers now spend less time typing syntax and more time directing AI agents, reviewing designs and orchestrating systems. A good engineer with Claude Code, Codex or Cursor can finish in an afternoon what used to take a week.</p>
<p class="mt-4">The way we hire outside help has not caught up. On most freelance marketplaces you still write a job post by hand, read dozens of proposals that all look alike, and then lose days sharing repositories, zipping files and setting up environments before any real work begins. The hiring happens in one place, and the work happens somewhere else entirely.</p>
<p class="mt-4">Today we're introducing the <strong>Think4Ever Marketplace</strong>, an outsourcing marketplace built directly into the Think4Ever agentic design and coding platform. You can outsource a single task or an entire project without leaving Think4Ever. ThinkBrain writes the job post and scores the proposals, and your hire starts work inside your project with no repo sharing and no setup.</p>

<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">Two ways to work</h2>
<p>The Marketplace covers the full range of outsourcing, from a one-off fix to a complete product build.</p>
<p class="mt-4"><strong>Task freelancing.</strong> Turn a backlog item, a module refactor, a custom MCP tool, a custom application or any other task into a job listing, and hire a specialist for it. It's the fastest way to add capacity for a well-defined piece of work.</p>
<p class="mt-4"><strong>Full-project outsourcing.</strong> Hand an entire design and development project to an outside team or agency. You keep full visibility, audit trails and control over exactly what they can access, so outsourcing the work never means giving up governance of the system.</p>
<p class="mt-4">It isn't only for code. Clients post work across Development &amp; IT, AI Services, Design &amp; Creative, Sales &amp; Marketing, Writing &amp; Translation, Admin &amp; Customer Support, Data Science &amp; Analytics, and Engineering &amp; Architecture.</p>

<section>
<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">What makes it different</h2>

<h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3 mt-6">1. ThinkBrain writes your job post</h3>
<p>Most clients aren't specification writers, and vague posts attract vague proposals. On Think4Ever you describe what you need in a sentence or two, and ThinkBrain, the platform's AI assistant, drafts the job post for you: the scope, deliverables, budget and screening questions.</p>
<p class="mt-4">Say a product manager types: <em>"I need a microservice that processes Stripe webhooks and updates our workspace database."</em> ThinkBrain expands that into a precise post with the expected deliverables and technical screening questions for applicants. You review it, adjust anything you like, and post. You can also attach a PRD, a statement of work or designs.</p>
<p class="mt-4">If you connect a Think4Ever project, ThinkBrain reads it to make the post even more precise. <strong>Connecting a project doesn't share it with anyone.</strong> Nobody gets access until you offer a contract with access and the freelancer accepts it.</p>

<h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3 mt-8">2. Every proposal is scored against your requirements</h3>
<p>Instead of reading every proposal yourself, you get proposals that ThinkBrain has already scored against your requirements and your connected project, with an explanation of each score. You can go straight to the best fits, then message, shortlist or decline with a click. ThinkBrain does the first pass; you always make the final choice.</p>

<h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3 mt-8">3. Freelancers set their own rate and timeline</h3>
<p>Generic estimates assume everyone works at the same speed. That's no longer true. A freelancer who has mastered AI coding tools can deliver far faster than a legacy estimate would suggest. On the Think4Ever Marketplace, freelancers give their own rate and timeline, so prices reflect how modern, AI-accelerated work actually gets done, and skilled freelancers earn more for working smarter.</p>

<h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3 mt-8">4. Zero-setup onboarding, with access you control</h3>
<p>This is where the Marketplace differs most from a traditional platform. When you offer a contract, you choose:</p>
<ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mt-4">
<li><strong>the role</strong> the freelancer plays,</li>
<li><strong>the projects</strong> they can reach, and</li>
<li><strong>the type of access</strong>: a login to your Think4Ever workspace, a project-scoped MCP token they can use from Claude Code, Codex or Cursor, or both. Work that doesn't need your project can have no access at all.</li>
</ul>
<p class="mt-4">When the freelancer accepts, their access is set up automatically. There are no repos to share, no environment variables to send and no setup calls. They can start working in your project as soon as they accept.</p>

<h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3 mt-8">5. Your work stays in your workspace, and access ends with the contract</h3>
<p>Everything your hire builds lives in your Think4Ever project, with role-based access and audit trails. When the contract ends or the project is completed, access is removed and tokens are revoked automatically. There's no lingering account to remember to clean up.</p>
</section>
