<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">Overview</h2>
<p>You can add co-developers and collaborators to your Think4Ever account as <strong>company users</strong>. Each person gets their own login, one or more roles that set what they can do, and access only to the projects you choose.</p>

<p class="mt-4">Company users are sub-accounts under your Think4Ever account:</p>
<ul class="list-disc pl-6 mt-4 space-y-2">
<li>They don't need their own Think4Ever subscription or sign-up.</li>
<li>They work in your workspace and use your account's credits.</li>
<li>They see only the projects they own or that you grant them.</li>
</ul>

<p class="mt-4">Use company users for your own team: developers, designers, testers and business analysts. To hire outside talent, use the <a href="https://claude.ai/code/artifact/e89838c4-eafc-454b-addf-e43e5d8d96c3" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-blue-600 hover:underline">Think4Ever Marketplace</a>. A freelancer who accepts a Marketplace contract is added to this same user list automatically, with the role and projects set in the contract.</p>
</section>

<section>
<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">Roles and access</h2>
<p>Two settings decide what each person can do:</p>
<ul class="list-disc pl-6 mt-4 space-y-2">
<li><strong>Roles</strong> set <em>what</em> they can do. Each role bundles a set of permissions. If you give someone several roles, they get every permission from all of them.</li>
<li><strong>Project access</strong> sets <em>where</em> they can do it. People see only the projects you tick for them.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">Step by step: add a user</h2>
<ol class="list-decimal pl-6 space-y-4">
<li><strong>Open your projects list.</strong> Sign in to Think4Ever and go to <strong>Your Projects</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Open the Users tab.</strong> Click <strong>Users</strong> next to Projects, API Keys &amp; Usage and Marketplace.</li>
</ol>
<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/add-co-developers-and-collaborators/add-co-developers-3.jpg" alt="Project List Users Tab" class="w-full h-auto" />
</div>
</figure>

<ol class="list-decimal pl-6 space-y-4 mt-4" start="3">
<li><strong>Review your company users.</strong> The <strong>Company Users</strong> page lists everyone on your account, with their username, email, name, roles, status and last login. You are marked <strong>ADMIN</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/add-co-developers-and-collaborators/add-co-developers-4.jpg" alt="Company Users list" class="w-full h-auto" />
</div>
</figure>

<ol class="list-decimal pl-6 space-y-4 mt-4" start="4">
<li><strong>Click Add User.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Fill in User details:</strong>
  <ul class="list-disc pl-6 mt-2 space-y-1">
  <li><strong>Username</strong> (required)</li>
  <li><strong>Email</strong> (required)</li>
  <li><strong>First Name</strong> and <strong>Last Name</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Password</strong> (required for new users). Share it with the person securely. They can't change it themselves.</li>
  <li><strong>Active</strong>: leave ticked so they can sign in</li>
  </ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Pick their roles.</strong> Under <strong>Roles &amp; Permissions</strong>, tick one or more roles. Each role lists its permissions so you can see exactly what it allows. Until you pick one, the dialog shows <strong>No roles selected</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Click Save.</strong> The user now appears in the Company Users list.</li>
</ol>

<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/add-co-developers-and-collaborators/add-co-developers-5.jpg" alt="Add User Dialog" class="w-full h-auto" />
</div>
</figure>

<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">Give access to projects</h2>
<p>New users see no projects until you grant them. Project access is set after the user is saved:</p>
<p class="mt-2">In the Add User dialog, the <strong>Project Access</strong> panel stays empty until you save the user:</p>
<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/add-co-developers-and-collaborators/add-co-developers-6.jpg" alt="Empty Project Access Panel" class="w-full h-auto" />
</div>
</figure>

<p class="mt-4">Once saved, open the user again with the edit (pencil) icon in the <strong>Actions</strong> column:</p>
<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/add-co-developers-and-collaborators/add-co-developers-7.jpg" alt="Edit User Icon" class="w-full h-auto" />
</div>
</figure>

<ol class="list-decimal pl-6 space-y-4">
<li>On <strong>Company Users</strong>, click the edit icon next to the user. The <strong>Edit User</strong> dialog opens.</li>
<li>Scroll down to <strong>Project Access</strong>. It lists every project in your account, with a count of the projects this user can reach (for example, <strong>3 PROJECTS</strong>).</li>
<li>Tick each project the user should work on. Its badge changes from <strong>NO ACCESS</strong> to <strong>GRANTED</strong>. Untick a project to revoke access.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/add-co-developers-and-collaborators/add-co-developers-8.jpg" alt="Project Access dialog" class="w-full h-auto" />
</div>
</figure>

<p class="mt-4">In the same dialog you can also change their roles or set a new password. Leave the password blank to keep the current one.</p>

<p class="mt-4">The user now sees those projects, plus any they create themselves, and can work in them with the permissions of their roles.</p>

<p class="mt-4"><strong>Example:</strong> to bring in a tester for your Airport Operations System, add them with the QA Engineer role and tick only Airport Operations System. They can test that project and see none of your others.</p>

<p class="mt-6"><strong>Hiring through the Marketplace?</strong> You pick projects the same way when you offer a contract. Under <strong>Projects the freelancer needs</strong>, tick the projects they should work on, then choose their role and type of access. The freelancer is added to Company Users with exactly that access when they accept.</p>

<section>
<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">What the new user sees</h2>
<ul class="list-disc pl-6 mt-4 space-y-2">
<li><strong>Their own login.</strong> They sign in at <a href="https://portal.think4ever.com" class="text-blue-600 hover:underline">portal.think4ever.com</a>, the same place as every Think4Ever user, with the username and password you set. They can't change their password themselves. To give them a new one, edit the user and enter a new password. Their account is separate from yours, but it sits under your company account.</li>
<li><strong>Only their projects.</strong> Their project list shows only the projects you granted and any they create themselves.</li>
<li><strong>Only their permissions.</strong> Features outside their roles aren't available to them.</li>
<li><strong>Your credits.</strong> Work they do with Think4Ever AI uses your account's credits.</li>
</ul>
</section>

<section>
<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4 mt-10">Manage and remove users</h2>
<p>Each user on <strong>Company Users</strong> has two actions:</p>
<ul class="list-disc pl-6 mt-4 space-y-2">
<li><strong>Edit</strong> (pencil icon): change their details, roles or project access.</li>
<li><strong>Delete</strong> (bin icon): remove the user. Think4Ever confirms with <strong>User deleted successfully</strong>.</li>
</ul>

<p class="mt-4">To pause someone's access without deleting them, edit the user and untick <strong>Active</strong>.</p>
</section>
