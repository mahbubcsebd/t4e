<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">Overview</h2>
<p>The Think4Ever Marketplace lets you hire freelancers and agencies, and lets developers find paid work, without leaving Think4Ever. It is built into the Think4Ever platform, so a hired freelancer can start work inside your project with no repo sharing or environment setup.</p>

<p class="mt-4">The Marketplace supports two ways of working:</p>
<ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mt-4">
<li><strong>Task freelancing.</strong> Turn a backlog item, a custom enterprise application, a module refactor or any other task into a job listing and hire a specialist for it.</li>
<li><strong>Full-project outsourcing.</strong> Hand an entire design and development project to an outside team. You keep full visibility, audit trails and control over what they can access.</li>
</ul>

<p class="mt-4">Think4Ever's AI assistant, ThinkBrain, helps at each stage. It writes the job description and screening questions from a short prompt, and it scores the proposals you receive.</p>

<p class="mt-4">This guide covers:</p>
<ul class="list-disc pl-5 space-y-2 mt-4">
<li><strong>For clients:</strong> how to post a job and hire a freelancer</li>
<li><strong>For freelancers:</strong> how to register, find jobs and send proposals</li>
<li><strong>For both:</strong> how access, contracts and delivery work</li>
</ul>
</section>

<section>
<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">How it works</h2>
<p>A job becomes a contract, and access ends when the contract does.</p>

<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/think4ever-marketplace/marketplace-1.jpg" alt="How it works flowchart" class="w-full h-auto" />
</div>

<p>The client posts a job, the freelancer applies, and the client offers a contract that sets exactly which projects and tools the freelancer can reach. The work happens in the client's workspace, and access ends with the contract.</p>

<section>
<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">For clients: post a job</h2>
<p>You can post a job in a few minutes. ThinkBrain writes most of it for you.</p>

<ol class="list-decimal pl-6 mt-4 space-y-4">
<li><strong>Open the Marketplace.</strong> Use Marketplace in the Think4Ever account sidebar, the Marketplace tab on your Projects page, or the Marketplace link in a project's toolbar.</li>
<li><strong>Start a job.</strong> Go to the Jobs tab and click Post a job.</li>
</ol>

<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/think4ever-marketplace/marketplace-2.jpg" alt="Jobs tab" class="w-full h-auto" />
</div>
</figure>

<ol class="list-decimal pl-6 mt-4 space-y-4" start="3">
<li><strong>Connect your Think4Ever projects (optional).</strong> In the ThinkBrain panel, click Connect and tick one or more projects, then click Done. This gives ThinkBrain context about the project so it can write a precise posting. It does not give anyone access. Access is granted only when you accept a freelancer and they accept your contract.</li>
</ol>

<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/think4ever-marketplace/marketplace-3.jpg" alt="Connect projects" class="w-full h-auto" />
</div>
</figure>

<ol class="list-decimal pl-6 mt-4 space-y-4" start="4">
<li><strong>Describe what you need.</strong> Type a short request to ThinkBrain, for example <em>"For my project I'm looking for someone who will create marketing videos."</em> ThinkBrain drafts the posting and asks a few follow-up questions to sharpen it. Shortcuts under the chat help with Draft the posting, Skills & budget and Screening questions.</li>
<li><strong>Click Apply to form.</strong> ThinkBrain fills in the job form for you.</li>
</ol>

<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/think4ever-marketplace/marketplace-4.jpg" alt="ThinkBrain drafts the job" class="w-full h-auto" />
</div>
</figure>

<ol class="list-decimal pl-6 mt-4 space-y-4" start="6">
<li><strong>Review and edit the form:</strong>
    <ul class="list-disc pl-5 mt-2 space-y-2">
    <li><strong>Title and Description</strong> (goal, context, scope, deliverables and acceptance criteria)</li>
    <li><strong>Category</strong>, such as Video & Animation</li>
    <li><strong>Experience level:</strong> Any level, Entry, Intermediate or Expert</li>
    <li><strong>Project duration</strong> and <strong>Hours per week</strong></li>
    <li><strong>Budget type</strong> (Fixed price or Hourly) and <strong>Budget amount (USD)</strong></li>
    <li><strong>Skills</strong>, separated by commas</li>
    <li><strong>This is a contract-to-hire opportunity</strong>, if the role could become full-time</li>
    <li><strong>Screening questions:</strong> up to 5, and freelancers must answer them</li>
    </ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Attach files.</strong> Click Attach files to add a product requirements document (PRD), statement of work, designs or anything else freelancers need to read.</li>
<li><strong>Click Post job.</strong> Or click Save as draft to finish later.</li>
</ol>

<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/think4ever-marketplace/marketplace-5.jpg" alt="Post a job form" class="w-full h-auto" />
</div>
</figure>

<p class="mt-4"><strong>No Think4Ever project yet?</strong> You can still post a job. Skip the Connect step and describe the work yourself, or attach your PRD. Before you give a freelancer access, create a project in your workspace so their work lands there.</p>
</section>

<section>
<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">For freelancers: register</h2>
<p>To work on the Marketplace, you need a Think4Ever account. Everything happens through it: browsing jobs, sending proposals, messaging clients and getting access to their projects.</p>

<ol class="list-decimal pl-6 mt-4 space-y-4">
<li><strong>Sign up for Think4Ever.</strong> The free plan (Think Free) is enough to use the Marketplace.</li>
<li><strong>Open the Marketplace.</strong> Click Marketplace in the account sidebar.</li>
<li><strong>Set up your profile.</strong> Open the My Profile tab and add your headline, skills and experience. Clients see your headline next to every proposal you send, along with your rate, your reviews and the number of jobs you've completed.</li>
</ol>

<p class="mt-4">A strong profile helps when ThinkBrain scores proposals for a client. The score weighs your profile and your answers against the job's requirements.</p>

<blockquote class="border-l-4 border-[#314865] bg-[#314865]/5 py-2 px-6 rounded-r-lg text-gray-900 font-medium not-italic my-6">
To confirm before publishing: the exact fields on the My Profile tab. The demo recording does not show the profile setup screen.
</blockquote>
</section>

<section>
<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">For freelancers: find jobs and send proposals</h2>

<ol class="list-decimal pl-6 mt-4 space-y-4">
<li><strong>Browse jobs.</strong> In the Marketplace, open Jobs -> Browse jobs. Search by keyword, or filter by category, budget (Fixed or Hourly), experience level (Entry, Intermediate or Expert) and newest first. Click Save search to keep a filter.</li>
<li><strong>Read the job.</strong> Open a listing to see the goal, scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, skills and any attached files. Jobs written with ThinkBrain usually describe the real product, its screens and its stack, so you know what you're bidding on.</li>
<li><strong>Send a proposal.</strong> Under Send a proposal, fill in:
    <ul class="list-disc pl-5 mt-2 space-y-2">
    <li>your message to the client: your approach and relevant experience</li>
    <li>Your rate (USD)</li>
    <li>Timeline, for example 2-3 weeks</li>
    <li>an answer to every screening question. All answers are required.</li>
    </ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Submit the proposal.</strong> Track it under My Proposals, and reply to clients under Messages.</li>
</ol>

<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/think4ever-marketplace/marketplace-6.jpg" alt="Browse jobs and send proposals" class="w-full h-auto" />
</div>
</figure>

<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">
<img src="/images/tutorials/think4ever-marketplace/marketplace-7.jpg" alt="Send a proposal modal" class="w-full h-auto" />

<p class="mt-4"><strong>Set your own estimate.</strong> Think4Ever doesn't calculate timelines or prices for you. You know how fast you work with AI coding tools, so price and schedule the job on that basis.</p>

<section>
<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4">Hire, give access and deliver</h2>

<h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4 mt-8">Clients: review proposals</h3>
<ol class="list-decimal pl-6 mt-4 space-y-4">
<li>Open <strong>Jobs -> My jobs</strong> and click your job. Proposals are listed under <strong>Proposals</strong>.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Re-score all</strong> to have ThinkBrain score every applicant against your requirements and connected project. Each proposal shows a score and Think's analysis, so you can go straight to the best fits instead of reading every one.</li>
<li>For each applicant you can <strong>Message</strong> them, <strong>Shortlist</strong> them, <strong>Offer contract</strong> or <strong>Decline</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<figure class="my-10">
<div class="w-full overflow-hidden rounded-xl border border-gray-100 shadow-sm">

<p class="mt-4">Access is optional. For work that doesn't touch your project, such as a marketing video, you can offer a contract with no access.</p>
<p class="mt-4"><strong>Payment happens outside Think4Ever.</strong> You and the freelancer agree on payment directly. The amount on the contract is informational.</p>

<h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4 mt-8">Freelancers: accept and start work</h3>
<ol class="list-decimal pl-6 mt-4 space-y-4">
<li>Open <strong>Contracts</strong>, review the offer and click <strong>Accept contract</strong>. You can also <strong>Message</strong> the client or <strong>Decline</strong>.</li>
<li>Think4Ever creates your access on the client's server automatically, with only the role, projects and access type the client chose.</li>
<li><strong>Workspace user access:</strong> enter the client's workspace from your Think4Ever dashboard.</li>
<li><strong>MCP access:</strong> open the contract to find the server URL, your token and ready-made setup snippets for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and curl. Copy the snippet into your tool to connect to the client's project.</li>
</ol>

<h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4 mt-8">Where the work lives</h3>
<p>Everything a freelancer builds stays in the client's workspace and project, not the freelancer's. When the contract ends or the project is done, Think4Ever removes the freelancer's access and revokes their token automatically. Either side can <strong>End contract</strong> or <strong>Report a problem</strong> from the contract.</p>
